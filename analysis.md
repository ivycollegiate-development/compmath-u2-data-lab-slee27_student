# Campus Threat Analysis

Audience: School IT staff.

## Chart 1: Timeline
The chart compares daily phishing reports and login success rates for school IT staff. Login success falls to 0% on October 24, 2026. Staff should investigate this day. However, the two lines use different scales, and the chart cannot prove that phishing causes login problems.

## Chart 2: Weekday vs Weekend
Weekdays average 7.98 phishing reports, compared with 2.88 on weekends. School IT staff should pay more attention during the school week. However, there are 44 weekdays and only 16 weekend days, so the weekend average is less reliable. More reports could also reflect more people using school systems.

## Chart 3: Login Success Rate
The median login success rate is 56%, and only 4 of 60 days are above 90%. School IT staff should investigate the 0% rate on October 24, 2026. This single outlier day drives the left tail. Before drawing a conclusion, staff need login attempt counts, system logs, outage records, and confirmation that the data is correct.
